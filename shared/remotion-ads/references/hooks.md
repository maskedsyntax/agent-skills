# Hooks

The hook is the first ~1.5 seconds. If it fails, the rest of the ad does not exist.

## Requirements

- Visually executable (not just a line of copy)
- Works with sound off
- Specific to this product and audience
- No unsupported claims
- No generic startup language ("reimagine", "unlock your potential", "meet the future")
- Pattern-interrupt: the first frame should not look like every other SaaS ad

## Generate

For each selected strategy, write at least 5 hooks.

Each hook record:

- line (≤ 7 words if text is used)
- visual (what the camera is doing)
- first frame
- why it stops a thumb
- claim safety
- scores (1–10): scroll-stop, visual, relevance, clarity, emotion, novelty

## Hook types that work

- Extreme crop of a real UI detail already in motion
- A contradiction the audience feels ("12,000 photos. You remember 9.")
- A result appearing before the product is named
- A familiar mess, framed too close to be a screenshot
- A challenge with a visible clock or constraint
- A withheld object: UI entering from outside the frame
- Identity: the viewer recognizes themselves before they recognize the app

## Hook types to reject

- Logo on a gradient
- Centered phone, fade up, app name
- Feature bullet appearing line by line
- Fake testimonial
- "Wait for it..." with no picture
- Stock lifestyle footage with no product

## Ranking

Pick hooks that score high on scroll-stop **and** relevance. A mysterious hook that does not connect to the product by second 4 is a bounce.

Select:

- 1 hero hook for the main cut
- 3 hook variants for later (Phase 14)

## Sound-off test

Mute the storyboard. If the first 1.5 seconds still communicate a feeling or a question, keep it. If it is just words, add camera or UI motion.
